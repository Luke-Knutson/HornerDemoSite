THE HORNER AUTOMATION DIFFERENCE

Horner Automation is a leading U.S.-based PLC manufacturer, specializing in both traditional PLCs and All-in-One PLCs with HMIs. Our products have a high functionality-to-cost ratio and FREE programming software. Horner applications span a wide variety of industries and provide high efficiency and intuitive machine control with built-in, edge device functionality.

Everywhere you look, Horner Automation controllers are there! We have availability, an option for every budget and incredible support and resources to help your project run smoothly!
